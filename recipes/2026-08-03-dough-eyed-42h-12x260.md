## FLOUR

### Biga flour — 1386 g (80% of total)

| Flour | Weight | W | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casillo Pizza Superiore | 693 g | W340 | 13.5% |
| Casillo La 8 Plus | 347 g | W350 | 14.5% |
| Casillo Aroma | 346 g | W280 | 13.0% |

### Refresh flour — 347 g (20% of total)

| Flour | Weight | W | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caputo Nuvola | 347 g | W270 | 12.5% |

Effective blend: **W ≈ 316, P/L ≈ 0.59.**

*Why this split:* Superiore and La 8 Plus carry the structure through a 20 h cold biga
plus a 17 h ball retard without degrading. Aroma contributes flavour complexity. Nuvola
goes in the refresh for extensibility and open crumb on the day.

*Optional tweak for more rim:* Aroma's P/L of 0.75 is what pushes the blend to 0.59.
Cutting Aroma to 208 g and raising Superiore to 832 g brings the blend to P/L 0.57 —
slightly more extensible, slightly less flavour depth.

## WATER, YEAST, SEASONING

| | |
|---|---|
| Biga water | 693 g (50% of biga flour) |
| Refresh water | 607 g |
| Biga ADY | **2.4 g** (0.35% of biga flour as fresh, ÷2) |
| Refresh ADY | **3.5 g** (0.4% of total flour as fresh, ÷2) |
| Salt | 52 g (3% of total flour — refresh only) |
| EVOO | 35 g (2% of total flour — refresh only, added at 16 °C) |

**Yeast rationale.** The proven build ran 0.54% total fresh-equivalent yeast across 51 h
of cold time. This build has 37 h, so the dose rises to ~0.68% to hold the same
fermentation budget. The increase is weighted toward the refresh, because the ball retard
was cut harder (24 h → 17 h) than the biga (24 h → 20 h).

Fresh → ADY conversion is **÷2**, matching the proven document.

A 0.1 g precision scale is essential at these weights.

## WATER TEMPERATURES

Friction factors are measured on the KYS Pro Baker 7: **biga 10 °C** (5 min slow),
**refresh 18 °C** (full mix).

### Biga — target FDT 27 °C (acceptable 26–28 °C)

> **Water = 81 − (room + flour + 10)**

| Flour temp | Room 22 °C | Room 23 °C |
|---|---|---|
| 17 °C | 32 °C | 31 °C |
| 18 °C | 31 °C | 30 °C |
| 19 °C | 30 °C | 29 °C |
| 20 °C | 29 °C | 28 °C |
| 21 °C | 28 °C | 27 °C |
| 22 °C | 27 °C | 26 °C |

### Refresh — target FDT 19 °C

> **Water = 76 − (room + flour + biga temp + 18)**

Table assumes biga out of the fridge at **5 °C**. If your fridge reads 4 °C, **add 1 °C**
to every value.

| Flour temp | Room 22 °C | Room 23 °C | Room 24 °C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 °C | 14 °C | 13 °C | 12 °C |
| 18 °C | 13 °C | 12 °C | 11 °C |
| 19 °C | 12 °C | 11 °C | 10 °C |
| 20 °C | 11 °C | 10 °C | 9 °C |
| 21 °C | 10 °C | 9 °C | 8 °C |
| 22 °C | 9 °C | 8 °C | 7 °C |

Refresh water lands cold. Chill it in the fridge ahead of time or hold ice water ready.

## STAGE 1 — BIGA

**Saturday 10:00 PM**

| | |
|---|---|
| Flour | 1386 g (Superiore 693 + La 8 Plus 347 + Aroma 346) |
| Water | 693 g at calculated temperature |
| ADY | 2.4 g |
| Target FDT | 27 °C (acceptable 26–28 °C) |
| Mix | 5 minutes slow (~80 RPM) |
| Ferment | 20 h cold at 4–5 °C |

### Before mixing

1. Weigh the three biga flours into the mixer bowl.
2. Probe room temperature and flour temperature.
3. Calculate biga water temp: **81 − (room + flour + 10)**.
4. Activate the yeast: take ~60 g off the 693 g, warm it to 35 °C, dissolve 2.4 g ADY
   into it, wait 5–10 minutes until foamy. Adjust the remaining water so the full 693 g
   averages your calculated target.

### Mixing

- Pour the 693 g into the flour bowl.
- Mix **slow (~80 RPM) for exactly 5 minutes**.
- Rough and shaggy is correct for a biga. Do not develop it.
- Probe in three different spots. Target 27 °C, accept 26–28 °C.

### Into the fridge

- Cover tightly.
- **Straight into the fridge — no room-temperature rest.**
- Note the exact time. You are pulling it at 5:45 PM Sunday.

## STAGE 2 — REFRESH

**Sunday 6:00 PM**

| | |
|---|---|
| Fresh flour | 347 g Caputo Nuvola |
| Water | 607 g at calculated temperature |
| ADY | 3.5 g (dissolved before adding) |
| Salt | 52 g (10-minute mark only) |
| EVOO | 35 g (at 16 °C only) |
| Target FDT | **19 °C** |
| Mix | ~20–24 minutes total |

### Bassinage — pre-split before starting

| Portion | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 364 g | added first, 0:00 |
| 2 | 121 g | slowly, mins 2–8 |
| 3 | 91 g | slowly, after Portion 2 |
| 4 | 31 g | reserved, with salt at 10 min |

364 + 121 + 91 + 31 = 607 g ✓

### Before mixing

1. **5:45 PM** — pull the biga. Break into rough chunks.
2. Probe room and flour temperature.
3. Calculate refresh water temp: **76 − (room + flour + biga temp + 18)**.
4. Pre-split the 607 g into the four portions and label them.
5. Dissolve 3.5 g ADY in a small amount of warm water. Set aside.
6. Weigh 52 g salt and 35 g EVOO. Do not add yet.
7. Weigh 347 g Nuvola.

### Mixing sequence

| Time / Temp | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Biga chunks + 347 g Nuvola + dissolved ADY. Add Portion 1 (364 g). Slow (~80 RPM). |
| 0:02 | Dough begins coming together. Add Portion 2 (121 g) slowly across 6 minutes. |
| 0:08 | Portion 2 in. Begin Portion 3 (91 g) slowly. Hold Portion 4. |
| 0:10 | Add 52 g **salt** + Portion 4 (31 g). Slow for 5 more minutes. |
| 0:15 | Switch to **fast** (~220–250 RPM). Probe in, monitor closely. |
| **16 °C** | Drizzle in 35 g **EVOO**. Continue mixing. |
| **19 °C** | **STOP THE MIXER IMMEDIATELY.** |

**Temperature is the stop signal, not the clock.** This batch is 50% larger than the
proven one and 5% stiffer, both of which change how fast friction builds. If 19 °C
arrives before the dough is developed, the water was too warm — write it down and we
adjust the friction factor next time.

### Fold, rest, ball

1. **6:25 PM** — rest 5 minutes in the bowl, lid on.
2. Tip onto a lightly oiled surface.
3. Three to four folds: stretch one side up, fold to centre, rotate 90°, repeat.
4. Shape to a smooth round mass. Lightly oil the top. Cover.
5. Rest 20–30 minutes at room temperature.
6. **6:55 PM** — divide into 12 pieces at 260 g on the scale.
7. Shape each into a tight smooth ball.
8. One ball per sealed container. Do not stack.
9. Rest 30 minutes at room temperature.
10. **7:25 PM** — all containers into the fridge.

## STAGE 3 — BAKE

**Monday**

| | |
|---|---|
| Out of fridge | 1:00 PM (≈17 h cold retard) |
| Temper | 3.5 h at room temperature, lids on |
| Stone temp | **430–450 °C** |
| Bake | 90–120 seconds |
| Disc size | 27–29 cm, rim 2–2.5 cm untouched |

### Tempering

Poke test from **4:00 PM**: press 1 cm into the ball. A slow 3–5 second rebound means
ready. Instant snap-back needs more time. A dent that stays means bake it now.

If the kitchen is at 24 °C in the sun, pull the balls at **1:30 PM** instead — this dough
carries ~30% more yeast than the proven build and a warm room will move faster than you
expect.

### Gozney Arc XL

- Preheat 30+ minutes on full, then **turn the flame down before launching** so the stone
  stops climbing. Read it with the IR gun, not the dial.
- Target **430–450 °C**, not the 500 °C the oven will happily give you. This dough carries
  2% oil, and oil at 500 °C scorches the base before the rim sets.
- The flame is at the rear. Turn every 20–30 seconds.
- **Stone recovery is your real constraint with 12 pizzas.** Each launch pulls heat out of
  the stone and a domestic gas oven does not recover instantly. Allow 3–4 minutes between
  pizzas and check the stone with the IR gun each time. Skip this and the last four bases
  come out pale.
